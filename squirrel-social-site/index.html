      Got questions?

      What is Squirrel Social?

      A social fitness game built around real-world movement. You move IRL, and the app turns it into XP, challenges, leaderboards and territory — with real people around you.

      Who can use it?

      Anyone who moves. Runners, walkers, lifters, cyclists, yoga people, weekend footballers. If you can move, you can play.

      Do I need to be fit?

      Nope. Challenges scale to your goals, and a 20-minute walk counts. Beginners and athletes play on the same map.

      What kinds of challenges are there?

      Three: daily goals you set for yourself, head-to-head battles against friends, and territory missions you take on with your crew.

      How does XP work?

      Every activity earns XP based on effort and consistency, not just speed. Streaks and challenges add bonuses. XP levels you up and unlocks badges and rewards.

      What is territory?

      Neighbourhoods, parks and campuses are zones on the map. Move there to build control for your crew. Most control claims the zone — until someone takes it back.

      Can I create challenges with friends?

      Yes. Pick a goal, set the time frame, invite your friends. The leaderboard does the trash talk for you.

      Can I join a crew?

      Join a crew near you or start your own and invite your people. Crews share XP, meetups and territory.

      When does Squirrel Social launch?

      October 2, 2026. Founding community members get in first.

      Is Squirrel Social free?

      Joining early access is free. We'll share full details on plans before launch — founding members hear first.
