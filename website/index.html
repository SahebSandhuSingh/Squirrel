    Got questions?

    What is Squirrel Social?

    A real-world fitness community. You move in real life — run, walk, lift, play — and the app turns that movement into challenges, territories, leaderboards and friendships.

    Do I need to be fit to join?

    Nope. Every step counts. Challenges scale to your goals, so beginners and athletes play on the same map.

    How do territories work?

    Complete activities in a park, campus or neighbourhood to claim it for your crew. Others can take it back — so keep moving.

    When does it launch?

    We're rolling out city by city. Join early access and you'll be first in when we land near you.
